What the assistant is doing

A typical menu bot

  • 8–10 taps through fixed menus: city, store, gender, service, date, time
  • Doesn't understand typed messages like "haircut tomorrow evening"
  • "No slots available, choose another date" ends the conversation
  • No reminders, no follow-up, and a client who drops off is lost

The VeloUp booking assistant

  • Books from one natural message, in English or Hinglish
  • Asks only for what's missing and offers the nearest free slot and stylist
  • Suggests add-ons and memberships at the right moment to raise the bill value
  • Sends reminders, asks for a Google review and books the next visit, which cuts no-shows
